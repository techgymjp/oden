[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
You run an oden stall, looking straight down into the simmering pot.
Six customers line the counter, the orders keep coming, and every item drops into the broth.
The catch? Oden looks exactly the same whether it's cooked or not. All you have is your skewer and a poke.

■ POKE IT. IF IT'S DONE, SERVE IT
• Tap a compartment to poke it with your skewer. If it's ordered and cooked, it goes straight to the customer
• Not cooked yet? "Not yet…". Nobody ordered it? "No order"
• Wait at least 1 second between pokes on the same item. Poke too fast and it doesn't count

■ YOU CAN'T TELL BY LOOKING
• 8 kinds of oden: daikon, egg, chikuwa, konnyaku, hanpen, fried tofu, beef tendon and cabbage roll
• Each kind takes a different time to cook, and it shifts a little every time
• Remember what went in and when, and you'll keep up with the rush

■ A POT THAT STACKS 3 DEEP
• 6 compartments in 2 rows of 3, each holding up to 3 items (18 in total)
• You can only poke the top item. The lower layers peek out from the edges
• When the pot is full, you can't take any more orders

■ DON'T KEEP THEM WAITING
• Make customers wait too long and they storm off, and your rating (♥) drops
• Bad pokes annoy everyone watching, and they get less patient
• Lose all 3 ♥ and the shop closes

■ SERVE SMART, SELL MORE
• Serve in a row for combo bonuses. Serve fast for bigger tips
• Each shift lasts 150 seconds. More customers and bigger orders as the day goes on
• Beat your best sales

■ ALSO
• You open with 6 items already cooked, and the first customers order exactly those
• Out of rating? Watch an ad to get ♥1 back and keep going (up to 2 times per game)
• Share your sales with friends
• Upbeat music and a different poke sound for every kind of oden
• Play offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese
